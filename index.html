INTRODUCTION

Hi! I'm Kaille. I like to do a lot of cool things! This is my website, it has all my favourite things, like my photos, my essays and links to all the places on the internet I take inspiration from and visit.

For now, with the lack of a better place to write my thoughts regarding this project, I will write what I am committing myself to below.

I like the idea of having a kind of 2000's, Windows XP System inspired website. I take a lot of inspiration from my childhood, experiences with the SEGA, the DS and GameBoy, and all those old retro systems. The colours remind me of my old primary school, and in more recent years this website has been inspired by my new fascination with MTG.

I have always had a fascination with games like RuneScape, and that "8-bit style" that comes from an authentically old and preserved part of digital culture. I hope you find my fascination with the aesthetic as enjoyable as I do!

I am looking forward to tinkering in here and seeing what comes of it. I can't wait to show you how it goes!
