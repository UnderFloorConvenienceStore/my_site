INTRODUCTION

Welcome to my website! It's a work in progress that will hopefully one day serve as a digital location for all the things that make me who I am.

Feel free to explore. To find out who I am, check the about section. If you want to leave a note, a suggestion or just want to say hi, head to the guestbook section. The archives section has all the things I've made, and if your interested in checking out some other digital spaces that I visit, check the links tab!

This website was built entirely through VS Code & Nano on my Linux system terminal. Using github and free, open source programs I was able to bring this website to life. I hope you enjoy it!
