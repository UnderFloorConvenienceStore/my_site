ABOUT

Hey! My Names Kaille. I'm so glad you came to visit my website, let me tell you a little bit about myself and about my workshop.

I am a recent UNSW graduate, coming away with a Bachelors of Science majoring in Mathematics and minoring in Psychology. Beyond that though I have a great interest in a lot of other academic fields. Philosphy, Psychology, Literature, Photography, History and Theology all bring their own little spark to my life of learning.

This website has taken inspiration from a bunch of amazing places, people, ideas and aesthetics. I have always had a love for the retro "16-bit" aesthetic of the 90's and early 2000's. When I was younger you would always find me on a Nintendo, SEGA, XBOX or PC playing something or messing around. I'm glad to say that all those hours didn't go to waste, because I believe thats what brought me here. I think of this website as a bit of a love letter to that younger Kaille. Bridging my two worlds together under one quaint little roof.

There is so much more I would like to share about me and this little world I am trying to create, but for now I am going to leave it at that.

If your're interested in getting to know me more, or want to have a chat you can find my contact information in my resume down below! Hope you enjoy looking around!
